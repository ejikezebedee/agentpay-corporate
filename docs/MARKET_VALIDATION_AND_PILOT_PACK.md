# Market Validation And Pilot Pack

## Purpose

Use this pack before running customer discovery, paid pilot conversations, agency validation calls, or partner proof-of-concept planning for AgentPay.

The goal is to validate demand, scope a narrow pilot, and collect useful feedback without implying that the static package is a live payment processor. Real payments, escrow, wallet custody, personal-data collection, or regulated activity still require legal review, compliance review, secure infrastructure, provider approval, and completed launch-control gates.

## Ideal Validation Targets

| Target | What to validate | Best proof asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder | Whether the product story solves a real MVP planning problem. | Website, console, roadmap, pricing pack. |
| Agency | Whether customization and implementation planning can become a paid service. | Customization workbook and implementation scope pack. |
| Marketplace seller | Whether the listing copy, screenshots, and buyer docs reduce objections. | Gumroad sales page and marketplace upload pack. |
| Payment consultant | Whether the architecture boundary is credible enough for discovery. | OpenAPI contract, schema, compliance pack, launch controls. |
| Enterprise operator | Whether the package supports internal review before production investment. | Due-diligence, privacy, security operations, and briefing packs. |

## Discovery Questions

1. What commerce, wallet, escrow, or marketplace workflow are you trying to present or validate?
2. Who needs to approve the next step: founder, client, investor, procurement, compliance, or technical lead?
3. Which part matters most today: website credibility, console walkthrough, backend planning, compliance boundary, or launch path?
4. What would make this package useful enough to buy, customize, or pilot?
5. What risks would stop you from using it for a client, investor, or internal demo?
6. What budget range is realistic for a starter package, paid customization, or implementation planning?
7. What production work would you expect after the demo is accepted?

## Validation Scorecard

Score each call from 1 to 5.

| Signal | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem urgency | Curious only. | Has a planned demo or client need. | Active project, client, investor, or approval deadline. |
| Budget fit | No budget. | Can buy product or small setup. | Can fund customization, pilot, or implementation planning. |
| Authority | Researching only. | Influences buyer or client. | Owns budget or approval path. |
| Use-case clarity | Vague interest. | Clear workflow but rough scope. | Specific workflow, buyer, deadline, and success measure. |
| Risk awareness | Ignores compliance. | Accepts production boundaries. | Wants proper legal, compliance, security, and launch gates. |

## Pilot Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Buyer understands the package is a starter, not a live payment processor.
- [ ] Use case is narrow enough for a static demo or planning pilot.
- [ ] Success measure is written before work begins.
- [ ] Sensitive data will not be collected through the static demo.
- [ ] Real payment, custody, escrow, and wallet activity are excluded unless production gates are complete.
- [ ] Legal, compliance, privacy, and security review needs are disclosed.
- [ ] License tier matches the buyer, agency, client, or reseller use case.
- [ ] Follow-up action is documented after the validation call.

# Revenue Operations Tracker Pack

## Purpose

Use this pack to manage sales, support, upsells, and maintenance opportunities after the AgentPay package is listed or delivered. It gives sellers and agencies a lightweight operating system for tracking buyer interest, support risk, revenue opportunities, and follow-up without collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Core Pipeline Stages

| Stage | Meaning | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | Person or company asked about the package. | Send sales page, screenshots, and disclosure summary. |
| Interested | Buyer asked about use case, license, or implementation. | Send quickstart, license notes, and relevant pack links. |
| Purchased | Buyer completed digital product purchase. | Send onboarding and verification instructions. |
| Activated | Buyer opened files, verified archive, or ran tests. | Offer customization workbook or walkthrough. |
| Upsell qualified | Buyer needs setup, implementation, roadmap, or maintenance. | Use implementation scope and roadmap packs. |
| Support risk | Buyer is stuck, confused, or refund-prone. | Use support triage and customer success pack. |
| Retained | Buyer accepted maintenance, update, or advisory support. | Track monthly deliverables and renewal date. |

## KPI Dashboard

Track weekly:

- Views or inquiries.
- Purchases.
- Activation confirmations.
- Support tickets.
- Refund requests.
- Reviews or testimonials.
- Paid walkthroughs.
- Implementation quotes sent.
- Implementation quotes accepted.
- Maintenance offers sent.
- Maintenance offers accepted.

## Conversion Metrics

| Metric | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Inquiry to purchase | Purchases divided by inquiries. |
| Purchase to activation | Activated buyers divided by purchases. |
| Activation to upsell | Upsell-qualified buyers divided by activated buyers. |
| Quote close rate | Accepted quotes divided by quotes sent. |
| Refund pressure | Refund requests divided by purchases. |
| Retention rate | Active maintenance buyers divided by maintenance buyers due for renewal. |

## Weekly Revenue Review

Use this agenda once per week:

1. Count new inquiries and purchases.
2. Review buyers who did not activate.
3. Review unresolved support tickets.
4. Identify buyers who need implementation scope or roadmap support.
5. Send review or testimonial requests to healthy buyers.
6. Update maintenance and renewal opportunities.
7. Record any compliance, security, or payment expectation risks.
8. Decide next channel action: marketplace update, outreach, demo, affiliate, or content post.

## Follow-Up Message Templates

### Inquiry Follow-Up

```text
Thanks for checking out AgentPay. It is best for fintech-style demos, MVP planning, agency prototypes, and implementation scoping. It is not a live payment processor out of the box. If your goal includes real payments, review the compliance, security operations, and launch-control materials before production work.
```

### Activation Follow-Up

```text
Just checking whether you were able to open `index.html`, `app.html`, and the buyer quickstart. If you want to turn the package into a staged MVP plan, start with `docs/PRODUCT_ROADMAP_AND_BACKLOG_PACK.md`.
```

### Upsell Follow-Up

```text
If you want help adapting AgentPay to your brand, client demo, or production roadmap, we can scope a paid setup or implementation-planning package. The scope can include customization, roadmap planning, acceptance criteria, and launch-control review. Live payment processing, legal review, compliance review, and backend engineering are separately scoped.
```

## Risk Register

| Risk | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer expects live payment processing | Send disclosure, compliance pack, launch-control pack, and stop conditions. |
| Buyer cannot open files | Use customer success setup rescue flow. |
| Buyer requests resale rights | Send license tiers and reseller pack. |
| Buyer wants production backend | Use implementation scope and roadmap packs before quoting. |
| Buyer asks for regulated advice | State that legal, tax, and compliance review are not included. |
| Buyer reports security issue | Route through security operations and incident-response pack. |

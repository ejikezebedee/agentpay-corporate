# Partner Channel Enablement Pack

## Purpose

Use this pack when AgentPay is sold, introduced, or expanded through agencies, affiliates, implementation partners, referral partners, integration partners, or strategic channel relationships.

The goal is to help partners explain the product clearly, qualify opportunities, route implementation work, and avoid claims that turn a static MVP package into a live payment processor.

## Partner Positioning

AgentPay is a commercial starter package for an agentic commerce platform. Partners can use it to show a polished website, clickable console, backend starter, schema blueprint, API contract, sales materials, launch controls, and buyer handoff documents.

Approved positioning:

- "A ready-to-customize product starter for agentic commerce, escrow, wallet, and marketplace workflows."
- "A demo and implementation-planning package for founders, agencies, and technical buyers."
- "A structured foundation for product validation, investor conversations, procurement review, and staged MVP planning."
- "A frontend, console, backend starter, and documentation bundle that still requires production engineering before live transactions."

Do not position AgentPay as:

- A licensed financial institution.
- A live payment processor.
- A production escrow service.
- A compliance-approved wallet.
- A shortcut around legal, privacy, KYC, AML, security, or payment-provider review.

## Ideal Partner Types

| Partner type | Best use | Required boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web design agency | Brand customization, landing page deployment, demo polish | Do not promise live payment launch without backend scope |
| Product studio | MVP planning, roadmap, pilot package, investor demo | Keep production payments behind launch-control gates |
| Fintech consultant | Discovery, compliance planning, vendor review, implementation roadmap | Avoid legal or regulatory certification claims |
| Affiliate/referral partner | Introduce buyers and earn referral fees where permitted | Use approved copy and disclose referral relationship where required |
| Systems integrator | API planning, database implementation, deployment advisory | Verify credentials, infrastructure, audit logging, and compliance before production |
| Training partner | Teach founders or clients how to use the package | Present as a starter/demo product, not a live financial service |

## Partner Qualification Checklist

Before accepting a partner, confirm:

- They understand AgentPay is a product starter, not a live payment processor.
- They can explain static frontend, MVP console, backend starter, and production backend boundaries.
- They will use approved positioning and disclosure language.
- They will not resell outside the licensed tier.
- They know when to route buyers to implementation scope, compliance review, security review, or production launch control.
- They can support buyers without asking for private keys, payment credentials, seed phrases, or production secrets.
- They will keep buyer data, support records, and client files inside their own approved systems.

## Claim-Control Rules

Partners must not claim:

- AgentPay is regulated, certified, licensed, audited, insured, or bank-approved unless the buyer completes that work separately.
- The package can process real payments without production integration and review.
- The backend starter is production ready without durable databases, production auth, audit logging, payment reconciliation, secrets management, and operational monitoring.
- Refunds, revenue, investor interest, conversion rates, procurement approval, or compliance approval are guaranteed.
- Partner services include legal, tax, or regulatory advice unless provided by qualified professionals under a separate agreement.

## Revenue Share And Referral Notes

Use written terms before sharing revenue or referral fees.

Recommended fields:

- Partner identity and contact.
- Product or service covered.
- Referral window.
- Eligible buyer actions.
- Commission percentage or fixed fee.
- Payment timing.
- Refund and chargeback handling.
- Disclosure requirements.
- Non-exclusive relationship wording.
- Termination rules.

Keep partner payout records outside the buyer package.

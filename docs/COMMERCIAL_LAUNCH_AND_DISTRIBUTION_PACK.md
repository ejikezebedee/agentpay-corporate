# Commercial Launch And Distribution Pack

Use this pack to publish, promote, and track the AgentPay digital product after the buyer archive is ready.

This pack is for commercial distribution. It does not authorize production payment processing inside AgentPay.

## Launch Positioning

Primary offer:

AgentPay is a fintech-style product starter for founders, agencies, and operators who need a serious website, clickable MVP console, backend starter, database/API blueprint, deployment guidance, buyer handoff docs, and launch-control boundaries.

Best promise:

Save product-planning time and start from a structured commercial package instead of a blank page.

Do not promise:

- Licensed payment processing.
- Guaranteed revenue.
- Compliance approval.
- Production readiness for live payments.
- Legal, tax, financial, or regulatory advice.

# Executive Steering And Reporting Pack

## Purpose

Use this pack when AgentPay needs to be reported to founders, executives, board observers, pilot sponsors, procurement leaders, agency clients, or internal steering groups.

The goal is to turn demos, pilots, implementation planning, partner activity, and support signals into concise decision reports without overstating production readiness or regulated-payment capability.

## Steering Positioning

AgentPay can be presented as a structured commercial product starter with these decision lanes:

- Market-facing product story and sales assets.
- Static website and MVP console preview.
- Backend starter and production architecture blueprint.
- Procurement, privacy, security, compliance, and launch-control evidence.
- Pilot, partner, implementation, and account-expansion workflows.
- Revenue operations, customer success, and maintenance planning.

Do not present AgentPay as a certified payment processor, licensed wallet provider, escrow operator, regulated financial service, or production payment system until the required legal, compliance, security, infrastructure, and provider reviews are complete.

## Steering Risk Register

| Risk | Executive response |
| --- | --- |
| Product is mistaken for a live regulated platform | Repeat production boundary in all executive, buyer, and partner reporting |
| Leadership wants to publish before cleanup | Require final release checklist, checksum refresh, and archive integrity test |
| Pilot scope drifts into payment processing | Require production launch-control and qualified review before real transactions |
| Procurement asks for certifications not held | Use due-diligence evidence and answer only verified claims |
| Partner overstates capability | Use partner claim-control rules and documented handoff language |
| Support volume exceeds package scope | Use support policy, customer success triage, and maintenance boundaries |
| Commercial metrics are based on assumptions | Report only verified inquiries, demos, activations, quotes, and decisions |

## Executive Escalation Rules

Escalate to leadership before proceeding when:

- A buyer requests real payment processing, custody, escrow operation, or wallet balances.
- A procurement reviewer requests legal, compliance, security, or certification claims.
- A partner requests exclusivity, endorsement, production readiness, or commercial claims beyond documented open-source and service boundaries.
- A pilot sponsor wants production users, production data, or live settlement.
- A buyer requests custom work beyond the quoted scope.
- Support requests create recurring operational ownership beyond the support policy.
- Any document needs to reference live credentials, private infrastructure, or internal workspace paths.

## Final Steering Checklist

- Current product phase and archive status are recorded.
- Evidence pack references are listed.
- Buyer, partner, pilot, or procurement signals are separated from assumptions.
- Commercial opportunity is matched to a documented next step.
- Real-payment, custody, escrow, privacy, compliance, and security boundaries are visible.
- Owner, decision, and due date are assigned.
- Report avoids private paths, credentials, internal infrastructure, and unverifiable claims.
- Next executive update is scheduled only when there is a real milestone, blocker, buyer signal, or decision.
